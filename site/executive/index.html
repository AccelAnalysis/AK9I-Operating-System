High-Level Recommendation

Install a repeatable AK9I operating system that creates Organization, Consistency, and Growth.

Turn AK9I’s people, relationships, reputation, students, graduates, contracts, systems, and market presence into predictable outcomes.

1

Organization

Management Operating System

Create clear accountability and a repeatable way to run the company.
  • Confirm the functional work chart and reporting hierarchy.
  • Install Owner, Leadership, Staff, and Contractor meeting cadences using the Business Flight Plan.
  • Assign 3–5 controllable KPIs to each accountable function and standardize reviews, rewards, and discipline.
  • Make Connecteam the employee operating layer, connected appropriately to QuickBooks and Salesforce.
Result: Everyone knows what they own, who they report to, how performance is measured, and where decisions are made.

Consistency

AK9I Brand System

Make every AK9I customer and public touchpoint consistent.
  • Formally adopt the AK9I Brand Standards and controlled asset library.
  • Implement uniform, dress, and physical-environment standards.
  • Standardize events and outreach with approved materials, branded gear, messaging, and Connecteam readiness checklists.
  • Require staff and contractors to use approved standards, templates, assets, claims, and source-file controls.
Result: AK9I presents and delivers one recognizable professional identity everywhere.

Growth

Customer Pipeline — End to Beginning

Build growth from successful outcomes and relationships backward.
  • Activate Alumni, Contract Clients, and Employees as advocates.
  • Build loyalty and recurring revenue through scent kits, replenishment, webinars, continuing education, and repeatable services.
  • Strengthen student outcomes, placement, customer experience, contract renewals, and account expansion.
  • Establish Sales as a distinct function beginning at Consideration, with Salesforce controlling stage, owner, next action, and follow-up.
Result: Existing relationships create repeat revenue, proof, referrals, and a stronger foundation for acquisition.
