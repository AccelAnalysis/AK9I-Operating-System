# Meeting Minutes — Michelle Hartman & Jonathan Holman

**Date:** September 1, 2026  
**Organization:** AK9I  
**Participants:** Michelle Hartman, Jonathan Holman  
**Purpose:** Employee role-alignment interview focused on Michelle's actual responsibilities, Student Services, enrollment, compliance, student experience, and the customer pipeline.

## Discussion Summary

### 1. Role alignment and actual duties
Michelle described a role centered on student enrollment, VA/SCO responsibilities, ACCET-related follow-up, student communications, file closeout, and student-service coordination. The discussion confirmed that the existing job-description structure does not fully reflect how work is actually divided today.

### 2. VA / School Certifying Official responsibilities
Michelle described responsibility for student enrollment in VA systems, second certifications, terminations, responding to VA claims examiners and counselors, managing enrollment documentation, and maintaining student records. Ryan Gibbs has been cross-trained on portions of SCO work to provide backup coverage.

### 3. ACCET placement and post-graduation follow-up
Michelle discussed the 30-day employment follow-up process, employment verification, attestations, and the difficulty of obtaining responses from graduates. The process needs consistent monthly follow-up and documentation so AK9I is prepared for reporting or audit requests.

### 4. Enrollment-to-start attrition
Michelle identified the period between enrollment and class start as a significant risk point. Missing fees, diplomas, transcripts, and other documents can serve as early indicators that a student may not ultimately attend. Proactive contact should begin well before the class start date.

### 5. Salesforce and payment workflow
The discussion covered using Salesforce-linked payment links for administrative fees, dorm fees, scent kits, and other charges. The intent is to reduce manual follow-up while preserving the paid-invoice records required in student files.

### 6. Graduation and outboarding redesign
Michelle described graduation week as historically compressed and administratively difficult because instructors may still be finalizing exams, certifications, and student paperwork. A better model would move final academic paperwork earlier in the week and use the remaining time for outboarding, dorm walkthroughs, canine checks, equipment/crate return, alumni enrollment, career-readiness review, and a meaningful graduation event.

### 7. Alumni and advocacy
The group discussed rebuilding alumni engagement through guest speakers, testimonials, alumni-network enrollment, continuing education, referrals, recurring scent-kit relationships, and stronger post-graduation communication. Michelle emphasized that former students can be powerful advocates when they feel AK9I invested in their success.

### 8. Career readiness before graduation
Michelle recommended an earlier career-readiness checkpoint, around the point when students have achieved meaningful certification progress. Resume preparation, training videos, job-search activity, and interview readiness should begin before graduation week.

### 9. Curriculum and canine-care content
Michelle suggested strengthening practical canine-care education, including kennel sanitation, health checks, animal husbandry, preventive care, procurement basics, and related topics. Some content may fit in the core curriculum while more specialized subjects may be appropriate for graduate continuing education.

### 10. Branding, uniforms, and student culture
Michelle supported a consistent brand standard across logos, uniforms, merchandise, and communications. The discussion connected branding to the broader student experience and culture rather than treating it as only a graphic-design issue.

### 11. Student-services role and staffing structure
Michelle expressed willingness to take on meaningful work that aligns with her expertise, including student-facing coordination and selected contract/administrative responsibilities. The discussion supported rebuilding the role around actual needed functions rather than forcing work into outdated titles.

## Decisions / Conclusions

- Rebuild Michelle's job description around actual Student Services, enrollment, VA/SCO, ACCET, student communications, and graduation/outboarding responsibilities.
- Continue cross-training Ryan for defined backup tasks while preserving clear ownership.
- Create a more deliberate enrollment-to-start nurture process.
- Redesign graduation week so administrative closeout occurs earlier and graduation becomes a structured transition rather than a last-minute paperwork rush.
- Strengthen alumni, placement, referral, testimonial, and continuing-education activity as part of the customer pipeline.
- Clarify which responsibilities belong to Student Services versus Training, Administration, Sales, and Operations.
