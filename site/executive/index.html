Organization

Install the AK9I Management Operating System.

Create clear accountability and a repeatable way to run the company.

01

Confirm the functional work chart and reporting hierarchy. Establish who owns each function, what belongs within it, and who reports to whom.

02

Install the management cadence. Use Owner, Leadership, Staff, and Contractor meetings through the Business Flight Plan methodology.

03

Manage performance through evidence. Assign 3–5 controllable KPIs to each accountable function and standardize reviews, rewards, and discipline.

04

Make Connecteam the employee operating layer. Standardize schedules, time, PTO, forms, checklists, training, and communication while connecting appropriately to QuickBooks and Salesforce.

ResultEveryone knows what they own, who they report to, how performance is measured, and where decisions are made.

Growth

Rebuild the Customer Pipeline From the End to Beginning.

Strengthen the value created from existing relationships before increasing acquisition effort.

01

Activate advocacy first. Develop Alumni, Contract Clients, and Employees as credible sources of proof, referrals, recruiting, introductions, and reputation.

Build loyalty through outcomes and recurring value. Manage graduate outcomes and placement, client satisfaction and renewal, service expansion, scent kits, replenishment, webinars, continuing education, refreshers, and other repeatable relationships together.

Strengthen training and service delivery. Improve the student and contract experience that creates the results, satisfaction, and trust required for loyalty.

04

Establish Sales as a distinct function at Consideration. Use Salesforce to control stage, owner, next action, follow-up, conversion, and handoff before scaling Awareness activity.

ResultLoyal relationships create recurring revenue, proof, referrals, and a stronger foundation for efficient growth.
