# Meeting Minutes — Peggy Stoltzfus, Nigel Rhodes & Jonathan Holman

**Organization:** AK9I  
**Source:** `Peggy-Nigel-Jonathan.txt`  
**Participants:** Peggy Stoltzfus, Nigel Rhodes, Jonathan Holman  
**Purpose:** Clarify operational transition responsibilities, establish management continuity, and identify immediate execution priorities.

## Discussion Summary

### 1. Departmental budgeting and financial guardrails
AK9I historically has not operated with departmental budgets. The discussion identified a need for practical planning guardrails by major function, including Training, Kennels, School Administration, Sales/Marketing, IT, and Finance. Nigel explained that the existing chart of accounts is organized primarily around cost of goods, maintenance/overhead, and G&A rather than operating departments.

A near-term goal is to use historical spending to establish reasonable planning ranges rather than immediately redesign the full accounting structure.

### 2. Business-development planning and reporting
The group discussed the need for a documented business-development plan, projected expenditures, and recurring reporting on contacts, opportunities, status, and next actions. Historical trade-show and recruiting activity should be reviewed so future events can be planned and budgeted rather than handled reactively.

### 3. Connecteam, uAttend, payroll, and field-work records
AK9I is using both Connecteam and uAttend for different portions of the workforce. The group discussed evaluating whether those tools can be rationalized and whether Connecteam data can be exported into a repeatable payroll workflow. Geofencing, clock-in/clock-out controls, handler scheduling, and training-record documentation were identified as useful capabilities.

The conversation also highlighted the need to keep handler training records and probable-cause documentation current and auditable.

### 4. Transition of Nigel's responsibilities
Nigel outlined several major functions that historically rested with him: HR administration, financial operations, IT oversight, insurance renewals/audits, and recurring government/compliance tasks. The central concern was not merely handing work off, but creating a controlled transition so essential processes continue without disruption.

The discussion recognized that Peggy cannot absorb all of these responsibilities without defined support, process documentation, and prioritization.

### 5. Ryan Gibbs as administrative/finance support
The group discussed using Ryan as dedicated support to Peggy for administrative and accounting-preparation work, rather than allowing her to be pulled repeatedly into unrelated operating gaps. Examples included payroll preparation, purchasing records, spreadsheets, and potentially restricted QuickBooks work after appropriate setup and training.

### 6. Shipping, payments, and student records
The scent-kit/order workflow was used as an example of avoidable administrative friction. The discussion supported standardizing shipping/handling rates, using Salesforce/Stripe payment links where appropriate, and preserving the invoice documentation required for student records.

### 7. Management operating system
The group agreed that AK9I needs clearer functional ownership, reporting lines, recurring management cadence, measurable expectations, and documented follow-up. A higher-level operating-system discussion was proposed to align leadership around these issues.

### 8. Three near-term organizational priorities
The discussion framed three short-term priorities for AK9I:

1. Install the AK9I Management Operating System.
2. Establish a consistent AK9I branding system.
3. Rebuild the customer pipeline from the end toward the beginning.

These priorities were presented as a way to impose sequence and accountability on a large number of simultaneous initiatives.

## Decisions / Conclusions

- Develop practical departmental/function-level budget guardrails from historical spending.
- Continue the transfer of finance and administrative knowledge from Nigel to Peggy, with documented processes and support.
- Evaluate Connecteam and uAttend against payroll, scheduling, geofencing, and training-record requirements.
- Protect dedicated administrative support capacity rather than allowing critical support staff to be repeatedly reassigned.
- Establish stronger management cadence, ownership, and accountability before adding more complexity.
- Preserve required student financial documentation when payment processes are automated.
